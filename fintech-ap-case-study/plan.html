01 · Problem

What we are solving

Problem statement

Finance teams at growing companies lose most of their AP time to copying, checking and chasing: typing invoices into forms, picking account codes, waiting on approvers who lack context, and fixing mismatches at month-end. Current tools digitize the paperwork but leave the work itself manual.

AP specialist

"Get bills in correctly without retyping them."

  • Capture every invoice from any channel
  • Code it right the first time
  • Stop chasing missing POs and receipts
Budget owner

"Approve in seconds, and know what I'm approving."

  • See budget impact and vendor history
  • Approve from Slack, email or phone
  • Only see what needs judgment
Controller

"Pay on time, protect cash, close faster."

  • No duplicate or fraudulent payments
  • Pay dates that fit cash and discounts
  • A clean, audit-ready ledger every day

05 · AI UX patterns

Pattern playbook

Patterns from AI UX Playground's catalog, applied to money workflows. Each card names where the pattern appears in Ledgerline and the misuse to avoid.

Confidence score

Trust

Every extracted field shows a confidence tier. Above 95% it is filled and quiet; 70–95% is filled and highlighted; below 70% is left blank with a question.

F1F2F6

Avoid: using a percentage in place of a source. Confidence complements citations; it never replaces them.

Citations & sources

Trust

Tap any field to highlight where it was read on the PDF. Ask Ledger answers link to the exact bills and ledger lines they used.

F1F3F7

Avoid: a footnote dump of sources nobody can inspect in context.

Smart autocomplete

Inputs

GL code, department and memo appear as ghost suggestions. Tab accepts one; "Accept all confident" accepts a batch.

F2

Avoid: committing a suggestion on pause without an explicit accept, or suggestions nobody can switch off.

Human in the loop

Collab

A named person must approve payment release, new payees, bank-detail changes and period lock. The agent stages; the person commits.

F4F5F6

Avoid: a gate on every trivial step. Too many confirmations train people to click through.

Intent preview (plan, then execute)

Agents

A payment run shows its plan first: which bills, from which account, on which date, total fees and discounts taken, and cash left after. The same applies to bulk holds and reclasses.

F5F7

Avoid: plans written as prose. Show a table the controller can edit line by line.

Autonomy slider

Agents

Admins set Suggest, Co-pilot or Autopilot per vendor, category or amount band. Defaults follow risk: new vendors start at Suggest.

F1F2F3

Avoid: one global switch for the whole company.

Autonomy budget

Agents

Caps on what runs unattended, such as "auto-approve up to SGD 2,000 per bill and SGD 20,000 per day for trusted SaaS vendors". Hitting a cap routes to a person.

F3F5

Avoid: hidden limits. Show the remaining budget where the admin can see it.

Self-correction

Agents

Before a bill is shown, the agent checks its own work: line items sum to the total, tax matches the rate, due date follows the terms. It fixes what it can and flags what it cannot.

F1F6

Avoid: silent fixes. Say what was corrected and why.

Error recovery

Agents

A failed payment comes with next steps: retry tomorrow, switch from ACH to wire, or email the vendor for corrected details (draft included).

F5

Avoid: an error code with no path forward.

AI context menu

Inputs

Right-click a bill or vendor: "Explain this variance", "Draft email to vendor", "Find similar bills". AI works on the selected object, not a blank prompt.

F7

Avoid: a menu of vague actions that open a generic chat.

Follow-up chips

Inputs

After Ask Ledger answers "Why is Q3 SaaS spend up 18%?", chips offer "Show the 42 bills", "Hold unused subscriptions", "Compare to Q2".

F7

Avoid: chips that trigger money movement without a preview.

Edit, reject, flag

Trust

Every AI output has visible controls to edit, reject or flag it. Flags go to a review queue and improve the model for that company.

All

Avoid: corrections that vanish. Show "Learned: Northwind freight → 5210" after a fix.

AI onboarding

Onboarding

First run imports 90 days of bills from the ERP, then shows what the AI learned: top vendors, their usual codes, and which ones it can already handle.

Setup

Avoid: an empty product that asks users to "try asking AI anything".

Autonomy levels in Ledgerline

Suggest

AI proposes values. The person fills or accepts each one.

Default: new vendors, bills over SGD 25k
Co-pilot

AI fills the bill and drafts routing. The person reviews and submits.

Default: known vendors
Autopilot

AI processes within policy and budget caps. People see exceptions and audit samples.

Opt-in: recurring, trusted vendors
06 · Agent rules

Agentic UX territories, applied to AP

AI UX Playground's Agentic UX framework covers seven territories. Here is the Ledgerline rule for each.

TerritoryQuestionLedgerline rule
PermissionWhat may the agent touch?The agent acts with the permissions of the person who enabled it, never more. It can never add payees or change bank details.
ReversibilityHow is an action undone?Everything is reversible until payment release or period lock. Irreversible steps always need a person.
AutonomyHow much runs unattended?Suggest, Co-pilot and Autopilot, set per vendor, category and amount band, with budget caps.
CostWhat does this action cost?Every payment run shows fees, FX spread, discounts taken or missed, and cash remaining before release.
Cross-app handoffsWhat happens outside Ledgerline?Slack and email approvals carry the full brief. ERP sync previews the journal entries before posting.
Ambient & voiceWhen does the agent interrupt?A daily digest by default. Real-time alerts only for fraud signals and payments at risk of failing.
TrustHow do users verify what happened?An activity log records each AI action with its inputs, sources, confidence, and who accepted it.

Anti-patterns to avoid

A chat box as the main way to use AI in a form-heavy workflow
AI that moves money without a named person releasing it
Filling a low-confidence field silently instead of asking
"AI-powered" marketing on a form that is still manual
Confirmation dialogs on every step, which train people to click through
Explanations that restate the answer instead of showing sources

08 · Metrics

How we measure success

North starTouchless rate

Share of bills that go from inbox to scheduled payment with no field edited by a person. Target: 40% by month 6.

EfficiencyMinutes / bill

Hands-on time per invoice. Target: under 4 minutes.

SpeedApproval time

Median time from submit to approve. Target: under 1 business day.

TrustOverride rate

Share of AI suggestions edited or rejected. Falling over time means the model is learning; a sudden rise means something broke.

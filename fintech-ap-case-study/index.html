Core insight

Most products already let you upload an invoice. Few of them read it. The PDF sits beside a blank form, and a person copies vendor, amount, dates and line items across by hand. The same pattern repeats downstream. Approvers see an amount but not the context, duplicates are found after they exist, and payment dates ignore cash position. Where AI appears at all, it is a chat box beside the workflow rather than inside it.

04 · Gaps

Seven gaps, with evidence

Each gap names the stage it sits in, how much it costs the user, the screens that show it, and the AI response proposed in section 05.

High impactG1 · Capture

The invoice is uploaded but not read

Airwallex, Melio and Acctual all show the invoice PDF beside a form, and the form is still blank. In Acctual the invoice reads $9.30 while the amount field reads $0.00. Airwallex's own Spend page promises AI that removes manual invoice entry, yet its bill form keeps seven required fields for a person to fill.

  • Copy-typing drives errors in amounts and due dates
  • Line items are skipped (Melio gates them behind Premium)
  • Email ingestion exists (Shopify, Acctual) but half the bills land as "Enter missing details"
Best in class: Mercury pre-fills amount and date from the invoice and adds a "Flag error?" control that feeds its bill scanner.
Response: F1 Invoice Autopilot
High impactG2 · Code

GL coding is a dropdown per row

QuickBooks opens a bill with empty Category and Description lines. Midday asks for a category on each transaction from a long chart-of-accounts list, and several rows sit at "No receipt". Nothing suggests a code from the vendor's history, and nothing explains why a code fits.

  • The same vendor gets coded differently by different clerks
  • Receipt chasing is manual
  • No bulk accept for confident suggestions
Response: F2 Smart Coding
High impactG3 · Approve

Approvers decide without context

Approval rules are built on one dimension, usually amount. Airwallex routes card expenses over 100 SGD to a manager; Acctual's rule is "amount ≥ $1.00, require 1 approval". At decision time Xero shows a one-click Approve button beside the amount and GL code, with no budget remaining, no vendor history, and no reason the item needs attention.

  • Low-risk bills wait in the same queue as risky ones
  • Rubber-stamping becomes the norm
  • Policy authoring requires thinking in rules rather than intent
Response: F3 Approval Brief & risk-based routing
High impactG4 · Duplicate & fraud

Duplicates are caught after they exist

Xero's Duplicate bill review lists vendors with two or three bills each and offers "Keep all" as the main action, so detection happens as cleanup after entry. Mercury is the exception: it warns "A similar payment has been created" inside the payment form. No product captured checks for changed vendor bank details, the most common invoice-fraud vector.

Response: F4 Guardrails
Medium impactG5 · Pay

Pay dates ignore cash and terms

Wise tells the user "You'll need enough money in your account to pay on the scheduled date". The cash risk sits with the person. Shopify Bill Pay lets you "Schedule 3 payments" in bulk but does not suggest when. No product captured recommends paying early to take a 2/10 net-30 discount or holding a payment to protect runway.

Response: F5 Pay Planner
Medium impactG6 · Reconcile

Matching waits for the next sync

Midday handles receipt matching well, and its message, "We didn't find a match yet — we'll check again when new transactions arrive", is honest. It is also passive: the user gets no candidate matches, no confidence, and no nudge to the person who holds the missing document. Airwallex prompts the user to connect Xero or QuickBooks, but exceptions still surface at month-end.

Response: F6 Close Agent
StrategicG7 · Across stages

AI lives in a side chat, not in the task

The strongest assistants on Mobbin (Klaviyo Composer, Shopify Sidekick) are general chat panels. Shopify's Sidekick sits in the same admin as Bill Pay but was not shown acting on bills. Finance users need AI that acts on the object in front of them, cites the source document, and leaves an audit trail.

Response: F7 Ask Ledger, plus AI embedded in F1–F6
05 · Solution

AI features that close the gaps

Ledgerline puts AI inside each stage. The model drafts and the person confirms. Every suggestion carries a confidence level, a source, and an undo.

F1 · Invoice Autopilot

Capture

Reads every inbound invoice from email, upload or portal. Extracts header fields and line items, matches the vendor record, and links the PO.

AI
Document extraction with field-level confidence; tap a field to highlight its source on the PDF
Human
Reviews only fields below the confidence threshold
Metric
Touchless invoice rate; minutes per invoice

F2 · Smart Coding

Code

Predicts GL account, department, class and tax code from vendor history and line-item text. Runs 3-way match against PO and goods receipt.

AI
Classifier trained on the company's own ledger; shows "why" (for example, "last 11 AWS bills → 6420 Cloud hosting")
Human
Bulk-accepts confident codes; corrections retrain
Metric
Coding accuracy; reclass entries at close

F3 · Approval Brief

Approve

A one-screen summary for the approver: budget left, cost vs. this vendor's average, contract terms, and anything unusual. Routing uses risk, not just amount.

AI
Generates the brief; scores risk; lets admins write policy in plain language ("Marketing spend over budget needs the CMO")
Human
Approves, rejects or asks a question in one tap
Metric
Approval cycle time; % of bills auto-approved within policy

F4 · Guardrails

Protect

Checks each bill before it is saved: duplicates by fuzzy match on vendor, amount, date and invoice number; bank-detail changes; unusual amounts or new payees.

AI
Anomaly detection across the vendor graph
Human
Must confirm a bank-detail change through a second channel
Metric
Duplicate payments prevented; fraud losses

F5 · Pay Planner

Pay

Proposes a pay date and funding account for each approved bill using the cash forecast, terms, early-payment discounts and FX rates.

AI
Optimizes a payment run: "Pay these 14 on the 28th, take $3,120 in discounts, keep 45 days of runway"
Human
Controller releases the run; money never moves without a person
Metric
Discounts captured; late fees; days payable outstanding

F6 · Close Agent

Reconcile

Reconciles continuously. Matches bank debits to bills and receipts, proposes candidates with confidence, and chases missing documents from the right person.

AI
Match suggestions; drafted nudge messages; exception queue ranked by close impact
Human
Clears exceptions; signs off the period
Metric
Days to close; open exceptions at period end

F7 · Ask Ledger

Everywhere

A contextual assistant that knows which bill, vendor or report is open. It answers with citations ("Q3 SaaS spend is up 18%; see 42 bills") and can act on a request ("Hold all unpaid bills from Northwind until the contract is renewed"), always showing a preview and writing to the audit log.

07 · Principles

Trust rules for AI that touches money

Show the source

Every extracted or suggested value links back to the document line, ledger history or policy it came from.

Say how sure it is

Confidence is visible per field. Low confidence changes the UI: highlight, ask, never silently fill.

People move money

AI can draft a payment run. A named person releases it. Bank-detail changes always need out-of-band confirmation.

Everything is logged

Each AI action records the input, output, model version and the person who accepted it, ready for SOX and external audit.

Undo is one click

Bulk actions preview first and can be reversed until the period is locked.

Autonomy is earned

Admins raise automation per vendor or category as accuracy is proven, using the ladder below.

Level 1

Suggest

AI proposes; the person fills the field.

Level 2

Pre-fill

AI fills; the person reviews every bill.

Level 3

Review exceptions

AI completes; the person sees only low-confidence items.

Level 4

Auto within policy

Trusted vendors flow through; sampled for audit.

09 · Plan

Roadmap

Phase 1 · Months 0–3

Stop the typing

  • F1 Invoice Autopilot at Level 2
  • F2 Smart Coding suggestions
  • F4 duplicate check at entry
  • Audit log foundation
Phase 2 · Months 3–6

Decide faster

  • F3 Approval Brief in app, email and Slack
  • Plain-language policy builder
  • F4 bank-detail change detection
  • Level 3 autonomy for trusted vendors
Phase 3 · Months 6–12

Run the close

  • F5 Pay Planner with cash forecast
  • F6 Close Agent and ERP sync
  • F7 Ask Ledger with actions
  • Level 4 within policy
Risk

Wrong numbers, confidently shown

Mitigate with per-field confidence, source highlighting and hard validation (totals equal the sum of lines, dates are plausible).

Risk

Over-trust by approvers

Mitigate by sampling auto-approved bills for review, and by stating what the brief did not check.

Risk

Data residency & privacy

Mitigate with per-tenant model isolation, regional processing, and no training on customer data without opt-in.
